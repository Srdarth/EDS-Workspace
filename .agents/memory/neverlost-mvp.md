---
name: NeverLost MVP direction
description: The initial product direction for the EDS ecosystem and the boundary of the first usable release.
---

The first usable NeverLost release is a browser-local scanner and diagnostic experience: it gives immediate visibility into a user's digital archive without uploading or opening file contents. The product should lead with clarity and a next productive move rather than presenting itself as a generic file catalog.

**Why:** The project's strategy prioritizes validating value quickly while protecting user ownership and privacy; deeper storage, AI enrichment, automation, and monetization come after this first signal of usefulness.

**How to apply:** Keep future features additive to the local diagnostic flow, preserve the "no upload" promise unless the user explicitly opts in, and connect each new capability to turning digital assets into an actionable outcome.
